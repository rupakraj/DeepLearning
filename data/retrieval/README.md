# Retrieval demo corpus

Six short news stories written in the style of Nepali English-language
newspapers, used by `embedding_retrieval.ipynb` as a toy document collection.

**These are teaching texts, not journalism.** They were written for this
classroom demo. The events, quotes, figures and people in them are invented,
even where the places are real. Do not cite them as reporting.

One file per story. The first line is the headline, the rest is the body,
one paragraph per line.
